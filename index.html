Letter mastery

Teaching order, not alphabetical. Greyed letters are not introduced yet. Top bar is the letter’s sound, bottom bar is big/little matching. A full bar means it is solid; only the sound bar advances the level.

Session length

Shorter is usually better. Ending while they still want more is the goal.

Level

Auto advances when every letter in the level is solid. Pin a level to stay put or to revisit.

Coaching guides

What to say, what to listen for, and the handful of things worth not doing. Also on the “?” beside each game.

Starting over
