Letter mastery

Teaching order, not alphabetical. Greyed letters are not introduced yet. Top bar is the letter's sound, bottom bar is big/little matching. A full bar means it is solid; only the sound bar advances the level.

Session length

Shorter is usually better. Ending while they still want more is the goal.

Level

Auto advances when every letter in the level is solid. Pin a level to stay put or to revisit.

Coaching guides

What to say, what to listen for, and the handful of things worth not doing. Also on the “?” beside each game.

Two devices at once

Keeps progress in step as you go, so ten minutes on the iPad and ten on your phone add up to one record instead of two. Letter and matching scores, the level, blending-by-ear, and these settings all travel.

Nothing about your child does. There are no names, no recordings and no words typed by anyone in here — only which sounds are solid and which need more practice.

Type the code shown on the other device. Connecting merges the two records — a letter is kept at whichever device practised it most recently, so nothing is lost either way.
